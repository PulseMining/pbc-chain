⚖ Principal Actions (this wallet owns the deposits)

Designate an heir who can claim your deposits after an inactivity period of 777,600 blocks (~18 months). You can cancel at any time to reset the clock.

🔑 Heir Actions (claim deposits from a principal)

If you are designated as an heir, submit a claim request. The deposits will transfer automatically after 777,600 blocks of principal inactivity.

How Inheritance Works
1. Principal Setup
The deposit owner designates an heir address. This resets the inactivity clock.
2. Heir Request
The heir submits a claim request on-chain. The 777,600-block countdown begins.
3. Auto Transfer
If the principal doesn't cancel within the window, all deposits transfer automatically to the heir.
Cancel = Reset
The principal can cancel at any time. This resets the inactivity clock and invalidates any pending claim.
⏱ What « inactivity » means — exactly
The countdown is 777,600 blocks (~18 months). It resets only when you perform an action signed with your own key: create a term deposit, withdraw deposit rewards, or update/cancel this inheritance setup.

Receiving funds does NOT count — mining rewards, incoming transfers, and deposit claims (even yours) do not reset the clock. This is deliberate: only an action you actively sign proves you are alive — a machine mining unattended must never block your heir.

If you perform any signed activity after your heir has submitted a claim request, that request is automatically cancelled and the countdown starts over.
